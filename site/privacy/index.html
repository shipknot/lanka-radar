Lanka Radar privacy policy

Effective 27 November 2026

In short: Lanka Radar has no account and collects nothing about you. Your places and settings stay on your phone, and ShipKnot has no server that could receive them.

Lanka Radar is an Android app by ShipKnot that brings Sri Lanka's official updates together: fuel, gas and market prices, exchange rates, gazettes, exam notices, jobs, holidays and power cuts. This policy explains what the app handles, why, and where any of it goes.

What stays on your phone

  • Your places for power cuts: each place's name, CEB area and point on the map, and the load shedding group the app finds for it.
  • Your settings: alert switches, quiet hours, your gas district and wholesale centre, and your recent searches.
  • What the app downloads: notices, prices and the PDFs you open, kept so the app works offline.

All of this is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read, and it is never sent to ShipKnot. If Android's own backup is on, Android may include it in your phone's backup, as it does for other apps.

Permissions

  • Internet: to download the latest notices, prices and power cuts.
  • Notifications: to alert you about new notices, price changes, power cuts and the reminders you switch on.
  • Location: only when you tap "Use my location" while setting up a place, to read your position once. Lanka Radar never reads your location in the background and keeps no history of where you go.
  • Alarms & reminders: so a load shedding reminder comes 30 minutes before the cut, not late.
  • Run at startup and in the background: to check for updates about every hour, and to set your reminders again after the phone restarts.

Where the app connects

Lanka Radar downloads public information. As with any website you visit, each server sees your phone's IP address and what was asked for:

  • Lanka Radar's data site (shipknot.github.io, hosted by GitHub): the notices and prices collected from official sources. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.
  • Official sources that only answer inside Sri Lanka, read directly by your phone: CEYPETCO for fuel prices, the Central Bank of Sri Lanka for exchange rates and market prices, HARTI for wholesale prices, the Public Service Commission for government jobs, and the CEB's EDLCare for power cuts and load shedding. EDLCare also sees which CEB areas and load shedding groups the app looks up. Your places and your location are never sent.
  • Map: when you choose a place on the map, your phone downloads map images from OpenStreetMap's servers, which see the area shown. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy.
  • Links and PDFs you open: these come from the official sites that publish them.

What Lanka Radar does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No ads, analytics or tracking, and no crash-reporting tools.
  • No selling or sharing of your information. ShipKnot never receives your places, your location or your settings.

If you allow Android to share usage and diagnostics with Google, Google Play may give ShipKnot crash and performance reports about the app. These reports do not include your places.

Sharing

Long-pressing a notice, a price or a power cut shares it as text through the app you choose. A shared power cut names the CEB area, never your place.

Keeping and deleting your data

Places stay until you delete them in the app, and kept PDFs until you clear them under Offline PDFs. Uninstalling Lanka Radar, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes everything it stored. ShipKnot holds no copy.

Not a government app

Lanka Radar is an independent app by ShipKnot. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Government of Sri Lanka or any of the sources above. Every notice links to its official publication, which is the one to rely on.

Children

Lanka Radar is not directed at children under 13, and ShipKnot does not collect personal information through the app.

Open source

The app's code, including how it reads each source, is public at github.com/shipknot/lanka-radar.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
