A trading licence shall be applicable only to – (a) entities engaged in the resale of electricity within condominiums and high-rise buildings where such resale is confined to specified premises, assets, or consumers; (b) entities engaged in the resale of electricity through electric vehicle charging stations; and (c) entities engaged in the purchase, storage, and subsequent resale of electricity through Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS). 4. Every application submitted under these regulations shall accompany a signed declaration in the form as specified in Schedule I to these regulations, and such application shall be made to the Public Utilities Commission of Sri Lanka (hereinafter referred to as the “Regulator”). 5. The Regulator shall have the power at its sole discretion at any time after the receipt of an application, to call upon an applicant to provide any information, documents, clarifications that it may, consider necessary on the contents of the application submitted, for the purpose of processing such application. 6. In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires: “Act” means the Sri Lanka Electricity Act, No. 36 of 2024; “applicant” means the person or body of persons who is or are making an application under the provisions of the Act for the grant or an extension of a licence, as the case may be; “high rise building” means a building structure comprising of independent units or parcels with common elements and shared services, served by a primary utility intake point and where common utilities including electricity are distributed, metered, or resold internally to individual unit owners or occupiers; and “Public Utilities Commission of Sri Lanka” means, the Public Utilities Commission of Sri Lanka established under the Public Utilities Commission of Sri Lanka Act, No. 35 of 2002. 9.
