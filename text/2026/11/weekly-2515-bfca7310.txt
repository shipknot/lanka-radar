DEPARTMENT OF LABOUR Open Competitive Examination for Recruitment to the Post of Stenographer - Grade III (Management Assistant - Non - Technical Service - Category 2) of the Department of Labour - 2026 RECRUITMENT will be conducted on open basis to fill the existing vacancies in the post of Stenographers Grade III (Management Assistant - Non - Technical Service - Category 2) of the Department of Labour, in accordance with the provisions of the Scheme of recruitment approved by the Public Service Commission. Applications are called from the Sri Lankan citizens who have fulfilled the qualifications prescribed in paragraph 3.0 below as at 14.12.2026 , the closing date of applications. 1. Posts of recruitment and the number of vacancies : Serial Designation Number 1 Stenographer (Sinhala Medium) 2 Stenographer (English Medium) Number of vacancies 09 01 1.1. Nature of the post/ posts: Permanent and pensionable. 1.2. Nature of duty of the posts : Approved Designation Tasks 1 Stenographer - Recording industrial court hearings in shorthand - Recording inquiries conducted at the Termination Division and Industrial Relations Division in shorthand - Recording shorthand minutes of Wages Boards Meetings in - Computer type setting and taking printouts of all work notes obtained as above and in other Departmental requirements - Other duties assigned by the Head of the Department in relation to the position. 2. Method of recruitment : This open competitive recruitment examination consists of a written test and a professional test. The written test will be held initially and the candidates who obtain more than 40% for that will be called for the professional test. The candidates should secure at least 40% of the marks allocated for each subject to be qualified for the appointment, as this is a competitive examination. The vacancies will be filled based on the number of vacancies to be filled, in accordance with the order of the total marks obtained. (Fulfillment of all qualifications prescribed in this notification is mandatory.) The examination is held in Sinhala and English mediums and applicants will not be allowed to change the medium of examination later. The candidates may sit for the written test in any medium they prefer.
