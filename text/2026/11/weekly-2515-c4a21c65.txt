Jalatheepan, Registrar General. ___________________________ MINISTRY OF HEALTH AND MASS MEDIA Recruitment On Open Basis to the Post of Nutritionist In Grade II of The Field/Office-Based Officer Segment 02, Service Category (MN 05-2006 A) - 2026 1.0. Applications are called from applicants who possess the following qualifications for the recruitment on an open basis to 29 posts of Nutritionist in Grade II , belonging to Field/Office Based Officer Segment 02, Service Category (MN 05-2006 A), for hospitals and institutions under the purview of the Ministry of Health and Mass Media. 2.0. Method of Recruitment Among the applicants who fulfill the qualifications specified in this notification, candidates who obtain the highest marks at the written examination conducted by the Secretary, Ministry of Health and Mass Media, or an institution approved by him, will be called for a general interview to examine physical fitness and qualifications and will be recruited according to the number of vacancies. 3.0. The number of candidates to be appointed based on the existing vacancies and the effective date of appointments shall be determined by the Secretary, Ministry of Health and Mass Media. The Secretary, Ministry of Health and Mass Media shall have the authority to fill/ not to fill any number of vacancies or all the vacancies. 4.0. Medium of Language This examination will be conducted in Sinhala, Tamil and English mediums. Candidates may sit for the examination in only one medium of their choice, and the medium applied for cannot be changed thereafter. 5.0. Applicants applying for this post should possess the following qualifications. 5.1. Basic Qualifications I. The applicant should be a citizen of Sri Lanka. II. The applicant should be of excellent character. III.
