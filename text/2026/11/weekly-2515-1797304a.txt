The acceptance of applications will open at 9.00 AM on 16 th November 2026 and close at 9.00 PM on 11 th December 2026.  Officers currently employed in the public / provincial public service shall hand over a copy of the application to their respective Heads of Institutions to be included in their personal files. When called for the interview, a copy of the application certified by the Head of the Institution and a service verification letter shall be submitted.  Other applicants shall retain a printed copy of their submitted application and produce it at the interview, if required.  The applicant shall endure any adverse consequences arising from delaying the submission of applications until the closing date. 1. The Commissioner General of Examinations, subject to the orders of the Public Service Commission, will conduct this examination in the main cities of all Provinces, in the month of February 2027. The Commissioner General of Examinations reserves the right to postpone or cancel this examination subject to the orders of the Public Service Commission - The "Appointing Authority" mentioned in this notice means the Public Service Commission in respect of the Sri Lanka Administrative Service, Sri Lanka Agricultural Service, Sri Lanka Planning Service, Sri Lanka Accountants Service, Sri Lanka Engineering Service, Sri Lanka Architects' Service and Sri Lanka Scientific Service; and the Education Service Committee of the Public Service Commission in respect of the Sri Lanka Education Administrative Service. 2. Method of Recruitment : 2.1 Indicating the preference :- Since an applicant is eligible to apply for multiple services or all services depending on the qualifications fulfilled for each service, the applicant will be given the opportunity to sit for the question papers pertaining to each respective service only according to the preference indicated by the applicant in the application at the time of applying. N.B : - Applicants will not be allowed to change the preference produced by them in the application later. Requests claiming to obtain appointments in a service for which the applicant has not mentioned his preference will not be considered on any circumstance whatsoever. 2.2 Written Examination :- - This examination consists of two parts as follows. The First Part (Common Part) of the examination is applicable to all services. Only the applicants who obtain 40% of the marks or more for each question paper and aggregate marks of 100 or more for two question papers of the First Part (Common Part) will be called for the Second Part (Specific Part for each service) of the examination. - Only the First Part (Common Part) is applicable for the Sri Lanka Architectural Service and the Sri Lanka Scientific Service, whereas only the Aptitude Test (Intelligence Test) question paper of the First Part (Common Part) is applicable for the Sri Lanka Engineering Service.
