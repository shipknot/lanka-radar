Procedure of Recruitment Applicants who fulfill the qualifications specified in the notification will be subjected to a written competitive examination and an eligibility evaluation interview and initially recruitment to a one-year training period will be made in the order of merit based on the total marks obtained at the written competitive examination and the eligibility evaluation interview, and according to the number of vacancies available. In the event of candidates scoring equal marks exceeding the number of vacancies, the decision regarding filling such vacancies shall be made in accordance with the orders of the Public Services Commission pursuant to Section 92 of the Procedural Rules. Candidates will be appointed to Grade III of the Basic Technician posts upon successful completion of a written and practical examination at the end of the training. Those who fail the post-training examination on the first attempt will be given 02 further opportunities to sit for the re-conducted post-training examination, and those who fail therein will have their services terminated. 6.
