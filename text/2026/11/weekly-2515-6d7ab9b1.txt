(IIA) - GAZETTE OF THE DEMOCRATIC SOCIALIST REPUBLIC OF SRI LANKA - 13.11.2026 MINISTRY OF FISHERIES, AQUATIC AND OCEAN RESOURCES Competitive Examination for Recruitment on an open basis to the Post of Junior Radio Officer Trainee Grade in the Management Assistant Technical Category - Segment 3 of the Department of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources - 2026 APPLICATIONS are invited from eligible citizens of Sri Lanka for the Competitive Examination for recruitment on an open basis to the Trainee Grade to fill 27 vacancies in the Post of Junior Radio Officer of the Department of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources. Recruitment will be made based on the results of the competitive examination and the said examination will be held in February, 2027 in Colombo by the Commissioner General of Examinations. The application has been published via "Online Applications - Recruitment/E.B. Exams" under "Our Services" in the website of the Department of Examinations, Sri Lanka www.doenets.lk and applications must be submitted online. Acceptance of online applications opens at 9:00 a.m. on 16 November 2026 and closes at 9:00 a.m. on 11 December 2026. Those officers who are already in the Public/Provincial Public Service shall handover a copy of the application to the head of the respective institution to enter in the personal file. A copy of the application certified by the head of the institution and a letter for the proof of the service are required to be produced at the time of calling for the interview. Any adverse consequences arising due to the delay in submission of the application until the deadline have to be borne by the applicant himself. 1. Functions assigned to the Post: This position involves dealing with the distress situations encountered by fishermen and message handling at regional fisheries offices, handling communication with fishing vessels, providing information for their vessel owners and for those who need it, maintaining the system established to provide information to fishers using state of art technology and ensuring the provision of information to them on a 24-hour basis. (Night shifts are mandatory). 2. Terms of Service: a) Once an applicant, who is selected subject to the general conditions governing the appointments of the public service, the Scheme of Recruitment for the Post of Junior Radio Officer in the Management Assistant Technical Category - Segment 3 approved by the Public Service Commission by its No.PSC/EST/7/1/36/1/2012 dated 14.08.2012, the amendments already made or to be made thereto in future ,and also subject to the provisions of the Establishments Code and the Financial Regulations, successfully completes the training period, he will be appointed to the Post of Junior Radio Officer Grade III. b) This position is permanent and pensionable. Although it was stated that this position carries a pension entitlement; the same shall be subject to the policy decisions taken by the government with regard to the pension scheme of the officers to be recruited in the future. You shall contribute to the Widows' / Widowers' and Orphans' Pensions Scheme. You are also required to make contributions to the same as determined by the government from time to time. c) Once these applicants who are entered into this Trainee Grade successfully complete the six (06) months' training course conducted by the Ocean University under the supervision of the Fisheries and Aquatic Resources and obtain the relevant certificate thereof, they will be selected to the Junior Radio Officer Grade III. d) This appointment will be subject to a probation period of three years. Appointees are required to successfully complete the first Efficiency Bar Examination within 03 years after appointment to the post of Junior Radio Officer Grade III as specified in the Scheme of Recruitment. e) The officers who are appointed Grade III subsequent to being recruited to a training period shall compulsorily serve 10 years in the Department of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources from the date of receiving appointments to Grade III. f) They must enter into an undertaking not to leave the service prior to the completion of said 10 years, and also agree that, should they leave the service before prescribed period, a sum recommended by the Director General of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources will be paid to the Department. g) Those officers in the Trainee Grade who will get through a written and a practical test held at the I fldgi (II w ) fPoh ‑ YS% ,xld m%cd;dka;s%l iudcjd§ ckrcfha .eiÜ m;%h ‑ 2026'11'13 4049 : P ART I : S EC . Should have passed the General Certificate of Education (Ordinary Level) Examination in six (06) subjects in one and the same sitting with credit passes in Sinhala/Tamil/English Language, Mathematics and two other subjects. And II. Should have passed three (03) subjects (excluding the Common General Test) in Science/Mathematics/Technology Streams at the General Certificate of Education (Advanced Level) Examination. Every applicant should be a citizen of Sri Lanka II. Should be of excellent character. III. Any person holding the position of a priest in any religious sect is not eligible to sit for the examination. IV. The qualifications required for recruitment to the post should have been complied with in all respects before the closing date mentioned in the notice inviting applications. 09. Age Limit: Age should not be less than 18 years and not more than 35 years as on the closing date of applications. (Accordingly, the eligibility regarding the age is satisfied only if the date of birth falls on or before 11.12.2008 and on or after 11.12.1991.) 10. Method of Recruitment: Recruitment to the Radio Officer Trainee Grade will be done based on the results of a written examination and an interview. Eligible applicants will be selected having interviewed a number of applicants in the ratio to the equal number of vacancies, in order of merits of the total marks of the applicants who have passed the written examination. If there are any apprentices who fail to successfully complete the training, they don't receive any chance to get their training period extended and accordingly their training will be terminated forthwith. 11.
