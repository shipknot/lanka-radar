# Google Play listing

What goes into Play Console for Lanka Radar, published by ShipKnot. The graphics are in this folder: `icon-512.png` (512 x 512), `feature-graphic.png` (1024 x 500) and five phone screenshots in `screenshots/` (1440 x 2560), rendered from the app's own screens with real data (the load shedding slot is staged for the sample place).

## Main store listing

**App name** (30 characters at most)

```text
Lanka Radar
```

**Short description** (80 at most)

```text
Fuel and gas prices, power cuts, gazettes, jobs and holidays in Sri Lanka
```

**Full description** (4,000 at most)

```text
Lanka Radar brings Sri Lanka's official updates together in one calm app, and tells you when something changes.

- Prices: fuel, LP gas, the rupee, market and wholesale prices, each with its history
- Notices: gazettes, exam notices and government jobs, in English and Tamil
- Dates: holidays, long weekends, exam days and application deadlines
- Power: load shedding and power cuts at the places you choose
- Alerts for only what you switch on, with quiet hours

Free, with no ads and no account. Your places stay on your phone.

SOURCES
Every item links to its official source:
- Gazettes: Department of Government Printing, documents.gov.lk
- Fuel prices: Ceylon Petroleum Corporation, ceypetco.gov.lk
- Exchange rates and market prices: Central Bank of Sri Lanka, cbsl.gov.lk
- Wholesale prices: HARTI, harti.gov.lk
- Exam notices: Department of Examinations, doenets.lk
- Government jobs: Public Service Commission, psc.gov.lk
- Power cuts: the CEB's EDLCare, edlcare.edl.lk
- LP gas: Litro Gas, litrogas.com, and Laugfs Gas, laugfsgas.lk
- Public holidays: Google's public Sri Lanka holiday calendar

Lanka Radar is an independent app by ShipKnot. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Government of Sri Lanka or any of these sources.
```

**Release notes** for the first release (500 at most)

```text
Lanka Radar's first release on Google Play: prices, notices, dates and power cuts from Sri Lanka's official sources, in one app.
```

## Store settings

- Default language: English (United States).
- Category: Tools, no tags. Free, no ads, no in-app purchases.
- AI asset declaration (asked when the listing is saved): don't label. The screenshots are the app's own screens and the graphics are drawn in code.
- Email: shipknot.dev@gmail.com
- Website: https://shipknot.github.io/lanka-radar/
- Privacy policy: https://shipknot.github.io/lanka-radar/privacy/

## App content

- Privacy policy: the link above.
- App access: all functionality is available without special access.
- Ads: no ads.
- Content rating: "All other app types". Online content: yes (the notices arrive after install), with no violence, sexuality, language or drugs in it; every other answer no. Rated 3+ everywhere (ESRB Everyone, PEGI 3, USK 0).
- Target audience: 18 and over. Minors are not blocked: the content suits everyone, and students may want the exam notices.
- News app: no. Government app: no (developed by ShipKnot, not for a government). Financial features: none. Health: none. Advertising ID: not used.
- Data safety: no data collected or shared. Places, their points and "Use my location" stay on the phone; the app only downloads public data.
- Countries: production in Sri Lanka only; the closed test in every country.
